Our Diocese

A Church at the service of the people.

The Catholic Diocese of Enugu was raised as an independent See on November 12, 1962, carved out of the Onitsha Archdiocese. From 17 original parishes it has grown into one of the most vibrant dioceses in Nigeria.

Under the pastoral leadership of Most Rev. Callistus Valentine Chukwuma Onaga, the fifth Bishop of Enugu, the Diocese continues to grow in faith, service, and infrastructure.

Diocesan Legal & Canon Law Services

Legal guidance, in the name of the Church.

These are informational services, not trade bookings. Reach out directly to the offices below for canon law matters, legal aid, or documentation guidance.

⚖️
Canonical Marriage Cases
Marriage investigations, dispensations, and canonical documentation — through the Chancery Office.
📜
Legal Aid & Justice
The Catholic Institute for Development, Justice and Peace (CIDJAP) offers legal aid and advocacy for the vulnerable.
🏠
Land & Property Matters
Guidance on parish and diocesan land documentation. Contact the Chancery for referrals.
🤝
Mediation & Dispute Resolution
Church-guided mediation for family and community disputes, in the spirit of reconciliation.
📋
To reach these offices: contact the Chancery Office directly, or the Catholic Institute for Development, Justice and Peace (CIDJAP), 3 Ikwuato Street, Uwani, Enugu. These services are handled case-by-case and are not booked through the form below.

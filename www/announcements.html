Pastoral Letter: 2025 Diocesan Theme

The Bishop has released his 2025 pastoral letter on the diocesan theme. All parishes are requested to incorporate the theme into liturgical and parish activities for the year. Copies are available at the Chancery.

Ordination

Priestly Ordination Announced

The Bishop has announced the ordination of new priests to the Order of Presbyter for the Diocese. Date and venue to be communicated. All faithful are invited to join in prayer and thanksgiving.

2025 Priestly Transfers Published

The annual priestly transfers have been published by the Chancery. All affected priests are requested to take up their new appointments by the end of the month. Parishes are to formally welcome their new parish priests.

Retreat

Diocesan Priests' Retreat — August

The annual diocesan priests' retreat will hold in August. All diocesan and religious priests working in the Diocese are expected to attend. Registration is at the Catholic Secretariat.

Jun 2025
Notice

Weekly Consecration to the Immaculate Heart of Mary

The Diocese continues the practice of weekly consecration to the Immaculate Heart of Mary. All parishes are reminded to include the consecration prayer in their weekly Sunday schedule.

Diocesan Pilgrimage to Aokpe

A diocesan pilgrimage to the Marian shrine at Aokpe has been announced. Pilgrims should register at their parishes. Buses will depart from the Cathedral grounds.
